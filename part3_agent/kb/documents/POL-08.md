title: UPI and Wallet Refund Timeline
category: refunds
---
Orders paid via UPI are refunded to the originating UPI ID within 24-48 hours of the refund being
initiated after quality check. Wallet payments are refunded almost instantly, usually crediting
the wallet balance within minutes. Both methods are faster than card or COD refunds because they
settle through real-time payment rails. Customers can track either refund from the order details
page.
