doc_id: POL-15
title: Refund Status Tracking and Escalation
category: refunds
---
Every refund is assigned a reference ID that customers can use to track its status from the
order details page. The status moves through initiated, processed, and credited stages, each
timestamped for visibility. If a refund is not credited within the SLA stated for its payment
method, the customer can escalate the reference ID to a human support agent. Escalated cases are
reviewed within 2 business days of being raised.
