---
doc_id: POL-05
title: Beauty & Personal Care Returns
category: returns
---
Beauty and personal care products, including lipstick and other cosmetics, are non-returnable on
hygiene grounds once delivered. The only exceptions are items that arrive damaged, expired, or
different from what was ordered. In those exception cases, a replacement or refund is issued
after photo verification. Opened cosmetic products cannot be returned for change-of-mind reasons.
