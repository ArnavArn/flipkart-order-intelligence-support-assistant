---
doc_id: POL-12
title: Reverse Pickup Scheduling and Failed Attempts
category: returns
---
Once a return is approved, the courier partner schedules reverse pickup and makes up to 3
attempts to collect the item from the customer's address. If all 3 attempts fail, usually because
the customer was unavailable, the system automatically switches to a self-ship fallback with a
prepaid shipping label. Customers are notified by SMS and app notification before each pickup
attempt. Failing to hand over the item within the retry window can cause the return to be
cancelled.
