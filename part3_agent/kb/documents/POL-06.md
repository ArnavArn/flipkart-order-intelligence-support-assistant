doc_id: POL-06
title: COD Refund Timeline
category: refunds
---
For cash-on-delivery orders, refunds are issued as a bank transfer since there is no source
payment instrument to credit back. The refund is initiated only after reverse pickup is completed
and the returned item passes a quality check. Once initiated, the amount reaches the customer's
bank account within 5-7 business days. Customers must share valid bank account details to receive
a COD refund.
