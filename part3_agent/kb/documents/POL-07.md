---
doc_id: POL-07
title: Prepaid Card Refund Timeline
category: refunds
---
Orders paid by debit or credit card are refunded to the same source card once the returned item
clears quality check after reverse pickup. The bank typically credits the amount within 3-5
business days of the refund being initiated. Actual crediting time can vary slightly by issuing
bank. No new bank details are required since the refund goes back to the original card.
