title: Delivery SLA — Non-Metro and Remote Pincodes
category: delivery
---
Non-metro towns generally see delivery within 4-8 days of dispatch, depending on distance from
the nearest hub. Remote or difficult-to-access pincodes can take up to 10 days because couriers
route through additional local transfer points. Weather and regional logistics disruptions can add
extra days beyond these estimates. Customers in remote pincodes are shown the estimated delivery
date at checkout before placing the order.
