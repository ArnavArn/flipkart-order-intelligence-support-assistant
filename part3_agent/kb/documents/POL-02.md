---
doc_id: POL-02
title: Footwear Return Window
category: returns
---
Footwear purchased on Flipkart can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Shoes must be unworn,
show no signs of outdoor use, and be returned in the original box with all accessories. Fit and
comfort complaints are accepted as valid reasons within this window. Footwear returned after
7 days, or showing wear on the sole, will not be accepted.
