---
doc_id: POL-14
title: Damaged or Defective on Arrival
category: returns
---
Items that arrive physically damaged or with a manufacturing defect must be reported within 48
hours of delivery. Customers are asked to provide unboxing photos or a video showing the damage
as evidence for the claim. Once verified, a free reverse pickup is arranged and a replacement or
full refund is issued without deducting any fee. Claims raised after the 48-hour window are
handled under the standard category return policy instead.
