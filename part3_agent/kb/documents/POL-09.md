doc_id: POL-09
title: Delivery SLA — Metro Cities
category: delivery
---
Orders shipped to metro cities such as Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Chennai are typically
delivered within 2-4 days of dispatch. Standard shipping is used for most metro pincodes with
same-day or next-day dispatch from the nearest warehouse. Express-eligible items in metro areas
may arrive even faster. Delays beyond 4 days in a metro pincode should be reported to support.
