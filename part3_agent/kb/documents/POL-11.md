---
doc_id: POL-11
title: Reverse Pickup Eligibility
category: returns
---
Reverse pickup is offered when the delivery pincode is serviceable by a courier partner, the
return is requested within the applicable return window, and the item is in its original,
unused condition. If reverse pickup is not serviceable at a pincode, the customer is asked to
self-ship the item instead. Eligibility is checked automatically the moment a return is raised
against an order.
